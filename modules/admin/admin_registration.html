Admin Verification Rules

All administrator accounts require strict validation before activation:

  • Full Name: Alphabets and spaces only.
  • Phone: Valid 11-digit BD number (01XXXXXXXXX).
  • Age Limit: Minimum 18 years old.
  • Password: Min 6 characters including 1 number.
  • Secret Token: Must start with ADMIN (e.g. ADMIN123).
  • ID Document: Upload .pdf, .jpg, or .png file.
  • Office Address: At least 10 characters long.
